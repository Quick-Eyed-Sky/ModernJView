ModernJView — How to launch the app
=============================================

This app isn't distributed through the App Store and isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer account.
As a result, the first time you try to open it, macOS will show a security warning.
This is normal — the app isn't dangerous, you just need to authorize it to run, once.

METHOD 1 (simplest, try this first)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Do NOT double-click it the first time.
2. Right-click (or Ctrl-click) on "ModernJView.app".
3. Choose "Open" from the menu that appears.
4. A warning window will appear ("unidentified developer" or similar):
   click "Open" to confirm.
5. Done! From now on, a normal double-click will work.

METHOD 2 (if Method 1 doesn't offer an "Open" button)
--------------------------------------------------
On recent versions of macOS, double-clicking may show a blocking message
with no button to continue. If that happens:
1. Open System Settings (Apple menu > System Settings).
2. Go to "Privacy & Security".
3. Scroll down to the Security section:
   you should see a message like
   "ModernJView.app was blocked to protect your Mac".
4. Click the "Open Anyway" button.
5. Confirm with your password or Touch ID if asked.
6. Relaunch ModernJView.app — it will now open normally.

Once you've authorized it this way, the app launches normally every time, just like any other app.
